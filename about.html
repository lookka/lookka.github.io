ABOUT THIS WEBSITE

Notes are a starting point,

Why this space exists

I created this website to collect the material I produce during my Master's degree in Theoretical Physics at the University of Turin. Rewriting and organising notes is part of how I study. Publishing the result makes it easier for me to keep it in one place and, I hope, may prove useful to other students too.

Alongside lecture notes, I may share personal projects, ideas, and reading suggestions related to the areas of physics that interest me most—especially theoretical physics.

Disclaimer

All notes and other material on this website are personal work, prepared by a student for study purposes. They have not been reviewed, approved, or endorsed by lecturers, teaching assistants, or the University of Turin. They may contain errors, omissions, or outdated information.

They are not a replacement for attending lectures, consulting course material, or reading the recommended textbooks. Please treat them as a reference or comparison point and verify any result that matters for your studies or work.
